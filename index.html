About

Glossary

  • Seat #: where a district ranks by how winnable it is for Democrats. Seat #218 is the one that would give Democrats a majority if they won it and every easier seat.
  • Chance Dems win: the average of four forecasts (DDHQ, Polymarket, Kalshi, Swingometer) as of 3:22 p.m. ET on Nov 2.
  • Majority line: 218 seats. Any seat ranked 218 or lower is one Democrats likely need.
  • Surprise win: a Democratic win where the forecasts gave under 40%.
  • Setback: a Republican win where the forecasts gave Democrats over 60%.
  • Pickup / Hold / Lost seat: a Democratic win in a Republican-held seat, a Democratic win in a Democratic-held seat, a Republican win in a Democratic-held seat.

"Held by" shows who holds the district number today (119th Congress). In states with new maps, a "Lost seat" may reflect redistricting more than a shift in voters. Several Democratic-held seats (such as TX-35, FL-09, AL-02) rank low, consistent with new lines making them harder.
